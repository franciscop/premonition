Premonition demo

Every page here is served with a 400ms delay, so the difference between a cold click and a preloaded one is visible. Hover a link, wait a moment, and the cached badge appears; click it and the page swaps with no reload.

What to watch in the console

  • once.js runs once for the whole session, it has data-pre-once.
  • every.js runs again on every page, it writes the two counters above.
  • ignored.js has data-pre-ignore, so it only logs on a real page load.
